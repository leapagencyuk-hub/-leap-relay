How to collect points? Bonus Points

Three ways, at different times, on different days.
(1) Double Blast

Double points for 30 minutes

You choose when to use it

Unlock one a day on diamonds earned, or earn one by ranking Daily Top 20 in Round 1, or by advancing a round.

The daily one expires at midnight. The earned ones you can bank to the Finale.

Round 1Round 2Round 3
(2) Bonus Hour

Double points for everyone

No card needed. Nothing to unlock.

21:00 – 22:00 local, every day of Round 1

Never use a Double Blast inside it. They cancel each other and you lose the card.

Round 1 only
(3) Match Points

Your daily highest match score is added to the leaderboard.

Only a complete match counts, over 5 minutes. Unlimited tries, highest one wins.

Cap: 10M bonus points a day

A match crossing midnight counts for the day it ends.
