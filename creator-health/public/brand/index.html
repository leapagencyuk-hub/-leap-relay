Rules

The arc appears twice, at most. The campaign band at the top of a page, and the one primary Fest action on it. Everywhere else that wants to feel like the campaign uses flat Fest Cyan. The gradient is the campaign's voice; spread it across progress bars and figures and it becomes wallpaper.

Cyan means earned. If a number did not come from LIVE Fest it is not cyan. That single rule is what lets a coach skim a page.

Red is a deadline, not an error. Behind uses #FF5F6D. Fest Red is reserved for when a window closes.

No money on a coach surface. Carried over from the portal. Coins yes, pounds no.

Numbers are tabular, always. A tracker updates every morning and a column that shifts by a pixel reads as broken.

Depth is a border, not a shadow. One hairline and one step on the surface ramp.

Fest colours sampled from the campaign key art in the 23 and 26 October agency recordings, reconciled against TikTok's published pair. Replace with the official asset pack the moment the agency toolkit lands.
