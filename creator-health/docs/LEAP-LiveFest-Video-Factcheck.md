## A. Material errors — fix before this goes to creators

### A1. "Going live between nine and ten is a double blast" — ~6:12
**Wrong, and risky.** 21:00–22:00 is the **Bonus Hour**, not a Double Blast.

- Bonus Hour is automatic. Every creator gets double points in that window, no card needed.
- It runs **Round 1 only, 20–24 Nov**, not across the whole Power League.
- TikTok warned explicitly: **a Double Blast cannot stack on a Bonus Hour. They cancel, and you lose the card.**

As phrased, a creator could burn a saved Double Blast at 21:00 and lose it for nothing.

### A2. "The 17th of November is when the Power League starts" — ~8:40
**Wrong.** Power League Round 1 is **20–24 Nov**.

17 November (23:59:59 UTC) is the **track cutoff** — the moment your Power League track locks,
based on your highest monthly revenue across the last six months. Two different things, and this
was said on the "three dates that cannot move" slide.

### A3. The third key date is missing / garbled — ~8:46
Said as "the fandom lock bands for the gaming as well". The actual date is **6 December**, and it
has nothing to do with gaming — Fandom bands lock on **active fan club member count**.

The three dates should be:
- **6 Nov** — Warm-Up opens
- **17 Nov** — Power League track locks
- **6 Dec** — Fandom bands lock

### A4. "Daily Quests" used for two different things — ~3:42, ~8:36, ~7:26
The video calls the Warm-Up's repeatable missions "daily quests", then uses the same name for the
real Daily Quest later. They are separate:

- **Warm-Up daily missions** — 6–19 Nov. Post a video (+100, twice a day) and go LIVE with the
  FEST effect (+50, once a day). 250 points a day.
- **Daily Quest** — **20 Nov – 21 Dec**. 3 LIVE missions a day, 30+ types, with the Lv1–Lv10
  reward ladder.

At 8:36 the video says that on 6 November we "start jumping in with our daily quest". Daily Quest
does not exist until 20 November.

### A5. "We recommend you do three of them" (Daily Quest) — ~7:28
TikTok's agency tip was the opposite and they stressed it: **treat 6 missions as the minimum**,
because Lv2 (6 missions) unlocks a **Double Blast you can bank to the Global Finale**. Three
missions only reaches Lv1, a treasure box. Recommending 3 leaves a free Double Blast on the table.

## B. Needs confirming before we say it again

### B1. "The gaming campaigns give you points in the main Power League" — ~2:55 and ~5:03
This is the strategic spine of the video and **neither TikTok meeting confirms it.**

What is confirmed: a **Power League Mirror Gaming Campaign** runs 20–26 Nov alongside Power League
Round 1, and there are gaming campaigns running all November. What is *not* confirmed anywhere in
my notes is that taking part in a gaming campaign awards points on the Power League leaderboard.

Power League points come from gifts, votes and bonus points. It is entirely plausible that the same
streams and the same gifts count toward both boards at once — that is probably what "mirror" means
— but that is an inference, not something TikTok stated.

Worth asking Carla directly, or at Daily Office Hours from 16 Nov, before creators plan around it.

### B2. "We've been told we have a very good chance of winning" the agency competition — ~10:07
The UKI+ Creator Network Tournament is real, and agency tracks are set on **July–September average
revenue**, so ours is already locked. But "we have been told we have a very good chance" attributes
a judgement to TikTok that, in my notes, is your own read of Carla's screen share ("we're in the
easy league, we've lucked out").

Our agency track sits on the same confidential calendar as the gaming campaigns. Saying it out loud
to 800 creators is a positioning call, not a factual error — but it is a promise we would have to
live with.

## E. Worth adding — the numbers that drive action are missing

1. **The 2,500-point target.** The video says "if you get a lot of points". The actual target is
   **2,500**, with **4,000 available**. Every creator on this roster can hit it. Without the number
   there is nothing to aim at.
2. **Warm-Up points carry 1:1 into Power League Round 1.** Finish on 2,500 and you start Round 1
   with 2,500 banked before a single gift is sent.
3. **The hard conditions on the daily missions.** The video says "post a video or go live with
   effect". The points only count with **#LIVEFEST2026 and the FEST effect** on the video, and the
   LIVE must be **over 25 minutes with the effect on**. Miss either and it scores nothing.
4. **Talent track must be SELECTED during the Warm-Up.** This is the biggest omission for a gaming
   roster. Having 50% gaming is not enough — the creator has to actively pick the Gaming track in
   November. Miss it and they cannot enter the Talent League in December at all.
5. **Fandom: each unique gifter = 100 points.** Twenty people sending a 1-coin FEST Pop is 2,000
   points before gift value counts. For a community-driven gaming creator this is the single best
   mechanic in the campaign and it is not mentioned.
