# The LEAP Portal: coach announcement

For `#staff-announcements`. Written in Perry's voice, no em dashes, ready to
paste. The one line that is not negotiable is the last section: coaches need to
hear that this is for them before they hear anything else about it.

---

## 🚀 THE LEAP PORTAL IS LIVE 🚀

CC @everyone

You all know the data has been there. Every morning the bot posts who is
slipping, who is close to 200k, who is about to rank up. The problem has never
been the data. The problem is that it is spread across ten cards in six
channels and you have to be a detective to use it.

So we built you a portal.

**One link. Your creators. Everything in one place.**

---

### How you get in

Click the link on any card the bot posts and sign in with Discord. That is it.
No password, no account to set up. It knows who you are and it opens on your
team.

You see your creators. Nobody else sees them, and you do not see anybody
else's.

---

### What is on it

**Urgent.** Who is slipping right now and still earning enough to be worth the
call. Not everybody who had a quiet week. The ones where a phone call this
morning actually changes the month.

**Protect the month.** Creators having a big month whose last week has come off
the boil. These are not emergencies and the portal does not treat them like
one. Lead with the month.

**200k graduation.** Who can still land it this month, closest first, and only
the ones who can genuinely get there at something like their current rate.

**Rank up and maintain.** Every creator with a bar showing exactly how far off
their next tier they are, what it is worth, and how much a night they need.

---

### The bit that actually saves you time

Open any creator and you get:

**What the data says.** Not "diamonds down 40%". An actual read: are the hours
down but the room still converting, which means it is time and not content. Or
are both down, which means it is the stream. Or has the fan club spend dropped
while the hours held up, which means it is engagement. Different problems,
different calls, and the portal tells you which one you are looking at.

**What to do about it.** The steps. Watch a live before you ring them. Check
they are welcoming and thanking. Is a gift goal running. Are they in a campaign.

**A message you can send.** Written out, with their actual numbers in it, ready
to copy and paste. No staring at a blank DM box trying to work out how to say
it at 11pm.

**Their TikTok, one click.** Go and look at the room. And there is a short form
check that tells you when they last posted, because that is the half of this
the LIVE data cannot see and it is usually the answer.

---

### Your money is on there too

Fixed, recruits, rank ups and diamond share, broken down so you can see exactly
what each part is paying you and what is still on the table this month.

Every creator in your rank up list shows what landing them is worth to you.
Because you should know what your effort is worth.

---

### Why we built it

To make the data accessible and readable for you as coaches.

You have always had the numbers. What you have not had is the time to go
digging through them, and nobody should have to. The portal does the digging
so you can spend your time on the part that actually moves a creator, which is
talking to them.

**This is a tool to make you better at your job. It is not here to replace
you.**

It cannot ring a creator who has gone quiet and work out that something has
happened at home. It cannot sit in someone's live and tell them the game is
not working. It cannot build the relationship that makes a creator pick up
when you call. That is all you, and it is the whole job.

What it can do is make sure you never miss the one who is slipping, never
forget who is 7,000 off a rank up, and never spend an hour working out what
the data is telling you when it can tell you in a sentence.

Use it every morning. Ten minutes, work the urgent list, send the messages.

---

### One thing to know

We can see who is using it. Not what you look at, not how long you spend, just
whether you are opening it. We are telling you that up front rather than you
finding out later.

It is not there to catch anybody out. It is there so we know whether this is
actually helping before we build more of it.
