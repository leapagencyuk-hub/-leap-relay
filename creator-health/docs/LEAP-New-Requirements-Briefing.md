# LEAP NEW CREATOR REQUIREMENTS
## Staff briefing — everything you need to know

---

## 1. WHAT IS CHANGING

From **1 November**, every creator who joins LEAP has 90 days to clear three
bars:

| rule | the bar | deadline |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Go LIVE at least once | day 7 |
| **2** | Go LIVE on 10 separate days | day 30 |
| **3** | Earn 10,000 diamonds | day 90 |

Miss one and they come off the roster.

### The shortcut

**Pass 10,000 diamonds at any point and they are finished early.**

The clock stops. NEW CREATOR comes off, CREATOR goes on, and rule 2 stops
applying to them entirely. A creator who goes hard in week one never has to
think about the day count again.

This matters more than it looks. A 90-day probation is something a creator
endures. A 90-day probation they can win in their first week is a race — and
nothing else in the rules gives anybody a reason to push early.

### Removal is not a ban

Anyone removed can rejoin and start again from day one. Say this out loud in
every removal conversation, because it is true and it changes how the
conversation goes.

---

## 2. THE BOT — WHAT IT NOW DOES FOR YOU

The LEAP bot runs the whole clock. You do not track dates, count days, or
remember who is due what.

**It handles:**

- Assigning **NEW CREATOR** the moment someone joins, with their 90-day clock
- Watching every creator's LIVE days and diamonds daily
- Sending the countdown messages (all six are in section 3)
- Flipping **NEW CREATOR → CREATOR** the second someone passes 10,000 diamonds
- Carrying out removals once a deadline passes
- Posting a daily checkpoint list to your channel: who is due what, this week

**What feeds it:** the monitoring service already tracks every creator's daily
LIVE days and diamonds. It publishes a checkpoint feed each morning — one row
per creator with a deadline coming, carrying their coach, which rule, what they
still need, and a verdict of `nudge` or `remove`. The bot reads that and acts.

**What this means for you:** you are not responsible for spotting anybody, and
you are not asked to judge anybody either. The bars are the bars. If a creator
on your roster is in trouble you will be told, and your job is to coach them
over the line before the deadline — not to argue for them after it.

---

## 3. EVERY MESSAGE THE BOT SENDS

Six messages. Each one goes before a deadline, never after. You will see each
one in your checkpoint channel as it fires, so you always know what your
creators have been told.

### Day 1 — welcome (everyone)

> Welcome to LEAP. Your 90 days start today.
> First one: get LIVE before **<DATE>**. It doesn't need to be long or polished
> — an hour counts. Just get the first one out of the way.
> Your coach is **<COACH>**.

### Day 5 — only if they have not been LIVE

> You've got 2 days to get your first stream in.
> An hour is enough. Tonight would do it.
> Need a hand setting up? Message **<COACH>** now, not tomorrow.

### Day 25 — only if under 10 LIVE days

> You're on **<N> of 10** LIVE days with 5 days to go.
> You need **<10-N> more nights**. An hour each counts.
> If that's not happening, tell **<COACH>** today and we'll sort something.

### On passing 10,000 diamonds — the good one

> That's 10,000 diamonds. You're done — you're a **CREATOR** now.
> No more clock, no more day counting. You proved it faster than most.
> Go and tell your room.

### Day 80 — only if under 10,000 diamonds

> You're on **<N> of 10,000** diamonds with 10 days left.
> That's **<X> a night**. Closer than it sounds.
> **<COACH>** can help you plan the last stretch — ask.

### Removal

> Your 90 days are up and the **<RULE>** wasn't met, so you're coming off the
> roster today.
> This isn't personal and it isn't permanent. If you want to come back, message
> **<COACH>** any time and we'll restart you from day one.
> Genuinely — good luck with it.

## 4. WHY THESE THREE BARS

We tested the rules against our own data before setting them, not after.

### They catch the right people

Had the rules been live, **58 creators would have been removed in September.**

| the 58 removals | |
|---|---|
| their combined September diamonds | **13,587** — 0.05% of the network |
| their combined October diamonds (first week) | **874** — 0.01% |
| how many streamed at all in October | **9 of 58** |
| how many are past 5,000 diamonds since | **0 of 58** |
| how many had already quit unasked | **9** |

**39 of the 58 did literally nothing all month.** No stream, no hour, no
diamond.

### The 30-day bar predicts who makes it

| first 30 days | still streaming 2 months on | earning 10k+ a month |
|---|---|---|
| cleared 10 LIVE days in first 30 | **59-71%** | **20-32%** |
| did not | **15-19%** | **0-2%** |

Creators who clear the 30-day bar are about **four times more likely** to still
be here two months later and **ten times more likely** to be earning properly.
That is the honest answer when a creator asks why the bar is where it is.

### This is not about money

Removing all 58 would have cost us a twentieth of one percent of the network.
It is about where your attention goes. Sur3shot is carrying 230 creators.
Nobody on earth can coach 230 people, and a large part of that list has never
gone LIVE once.

## 6. ROLLOUT

| when | what |
|---|---|
| **1 Nov** | Requirements posted and pinned in the creator server. Membership screening on — every new creator ticks them before they can post. Everyone joining from this date gets NEW CREATOR and starts their clock. |
| **Nov** | **Grace month for the existing roster.** Nobody who joined before 1 Nov is removed. They get the same countdown messages so they know exactly where they stand. |
| **1 Dec** | Rules apply to everybody. The backlog in section 6 clears. |

**Do not remove anyone from the existing roster during November.** Announcing a
standard and culling in the same week reads as a purge, and that will be the
thing people talk about instead of the rules.

---

## 7. EXCEPTIONS — PAUSING A CLOCK

Illness, exams, bereavement, a family emergency, a broken setup, a house move.
All of it is fine. All of it is pausable.

**The one condition: you have to flag it BEFORE their deadline.**

We can pause a clock. We cannot un-remove somebody gracefully — the message has
gone, the role has changed, and they have already told their mates they got
dropped.

If a creator goes quiet on you and you suspect something is up, flag it anyway.
A paused clock costs nothing.

---

## 8. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR RECRUITMENT

In steady state, expect **70 to 80 removals a month** against about **105
signings**.

We are not going to be a smaller agency. We are going to be the same size with
a roster that actually streams.

But it means the recruitment number that matters stops being *signings* and
starts being **signings that clear day 30**. Right now that is **31%**. If
recruiters are still measured on raw signings, they will sign 250 a month to
land the same 70 keepers and the rules become paperwork.

---

## 9. THE SHORT VERSION

1. Three bars: LIVE in 7 days, 10 LIVE days in 30, 10,000 diamonds in 90.
2. 10,000 diamonds at any point ends it early — clock stops, role flips.
3. The bot does all of it. Roles, messages, removals.
4. There is no appeal and no borderline. Hit the bars or you're off.
5. Flag anyone with a genuine reason — illness, exams — **before** their
   deadline. That is a paused clock, not an exception to the bar.
6. New joiners from 1 Nov. Existing roster from 1 Dec.

Questions in the coach channel, not DMs, so everybody sees the answer.
