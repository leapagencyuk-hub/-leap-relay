## 2. How agency tracks are assigned

**July to September average agency revenue → matched with similar agencies → confirmed in Backstage.**

> This is different from the tracks assigned to creators in the individual LIVE FEST competition.

The four agency tracks are **Diamond, Super, Gleam, Glow**.

**This is already decided.** Our track was set on revenue we earned between July and September.
Nothing we do between now and November moves it. What it changes is which rewards table we are
playing for, so it is worth knowing which one we landed in before we plan anything.

## 3. Getting creators signed up

> Warm-up points require **both opt-in and one 25-minute LIVE** during Warm-up. Awarded **once per
> eligible creator**; extra LIVE sessions do not add sign-up points.

In Backstage: copy the sign-up link or save the QR poster to share. Track who has signed up in the
creator list and message non-responders directly.

**Send the sign-up link or QR poster from Backstage, then follow up with creators who have not
opted in.**

This is the most countable job in the entire campaign. It is finite, it is once per creator, and
it is binary. Either they opted in and did a 25-minute LIVE, or they did not.

## 4. How agencies earn points in Regionals

**Creator diamonds + Milestone bonuses + Talent League share.**

| Source | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Creator diamonds** | Eligible diamonds generated by creators contribute to the agency ranking |
| **Milestone bonuses** | Reach agency-specific diamond targets within the designated time window |
| **Talent League share** | Music and Gaming creators earn points first. Agencies receive a % share of those creator points |

> Example: 10,000 votes = 10,000 creator Talent League points. Agency receives X% of that total;
> **X% is still to be confirmed** on the campaign page / Backstage.

**Read that third line again.** Our creators are gaming creators. The Talent League has a Gaming
track. Every point a LEAP creator earns in Talent League pays the agency a share on top. The
Gaming eligibility gate (50% gaming LIVE streams over the trailing 14 days) was already the best
route for our roster; it now has agency revenue attached to it as well.

### Agency-specific milestones

> Targets are agency-specific. Your agency manager will discuss targets once confirmed. Targets,
> deadlines and bonus points will then appear in Backstage.

We do not have ours yet. **Ask for them.**

## 7. The Network-led campaign leaderboard

This is a second, optional competition and it is easy to misread, so it gets its own section.

> **Running a campaign does not add points to the main leaderboard.** It is an extra push and a way
> to track how creators perform.
>
> Separate leaderboard · Mandatory templates · Manual creator selection

### Setting one up, with a two-person check

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1 Assign owner** | One person builds the campaign |
| **2 Select template** | Use the mandatory eligible template |
| **3 Add creators** | Manually verify the creator list |
| **4 Reviewer check** | Second person checks before launch |

> **Each eligible template is a one-shot setup. Review before launch.**

One shot. No edits. That is why they want a second pair of eyes before anybody presses submit.

### The dashboard it gives you

- **Sign-up by tier** — recruited creators, go-live status and tier mix for the campaign at a glance
- **Total points and live ranking** — the campaign score and its position against other Creator Networks in real time
- **Creator-level performance** — open the creator list to see each added creator's points and contribution

> Separate leaderboard only. Campaign points do not add to the main agency score.

## 9. Rules agencies should remember

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Creator status** | Departure stops future contribution. **Individual elimination does not automatically do so.** |
| **Milestone deadlines** | Use the target and deadline exactly as displayed in Backstage |
| **One-shot templates** | Add creators manually. Check participants and dates before submitting |
| **Escalate with evidence** | Share screenshots, creator handle, campaign and time with your agency manager |

The first one matters more than it looks. A creator knocked out of the individual Power League is
**still earning agency points**. Coaches will assume the opposite and stop working them, and that
is free money walking out of the door.

## 11. Said on the call but not on the slides

The audio degrades badly after about thirteen minutes, but the first half and the Q&A carry
numbers the deck does not. Flagged separately because they are spoken, so confirm each in
Backstage before anybody plans around it.

**A sign-up is worth 1,000 Backstage ranking points.** Per eligible creator, awarded only when
*both* actions are completed between 6 and 19 November. The figure is on the slide too, but
TikTok's own mock has a participant avatar burned over it in every frame, so 1,000 comes from
the presenter rather than from the page.

**It is paid once and only once.** Asked directly in the Q&A: *"If a creator opts in, they go
LIVE for half an hour one day, and the next day they go LIVE for another hour, do I get points
just once or..."* Answer: *"Just the once."*

**New creators recruited during Warm-up count too.** Anyone who joins the agency during the
Warm-up window and completes both actions earns the same points. Recruitment between 6 and 19
November pays twice: the creator joins, and the sign-up scores.

**There are two ways a creator can opt in.** They can go LIVE and opt in from the LIVE Fest
banner or tab in the app, or the agency can send the opt-in directly from Backstage. Backstage
also has a built-in check for who has registered and who has met the LIVE requirement, described
as "literally click a button" — in previous campaigns agencies had to work that out themselves.

**Gaming and music rosters are recognised this year.** Verbatim: *"We've heard you guys. This
time around we do have something recognising [agencies] that have a higher percentage of gaming
or music creators."* No detail was given beyond that, and it is the single most interesting line
on the call for us.

**Milestones come in at least two stages**, each with its own deadline and points target,
tailored to each agency on past performance. The worked example given: milestone one might
require 100,000 points by 20 November.

**A tracker is coming.** TikTok said they will share one in the upcoming toolkit.

**Every time on the deck is UK local time**, except the Global stage, which is UTC+0.

## 12. What this means for LEAP

**Our track is already locked.** July to September average revenue decided it. Find out which of
Diamond, Super, Gleam or Glow we are in, because it decides what we are actually playing for.

**Sign-ups are the one job that is fully in our control and fully countable.** Opt-in plus one
25-minute LIVE, once per creator, no credit for extra sessions. That is a tick-box per creator
across the whole roster and it is exactly the shape the portal already handles. This is the
highest-certainty agency points available and it closes on 19 November.

**The Talent League share puts agency money behind the gaming gate.** Our creators qualify for the
Gaming track on 50% gaming LIVE over the trailing 14 days. Every Talent League point they earn pays
us a share on top. The percentage is still unconfirmed, which is the single biggest unknown in this
deck for us.

**Milestone bonuses need chasing.** They are agency-specific, they come from our agency manager,
and we have not been given ours. Nothing else on this list is worth planning around until we know
what the targets are.

**An eliminated creator still earns for us.** Coaches need telling this explicitly, because the
natural assumption is the opposite and it would quietly cost us points through December.

**Do not start with Network-led campaigns.** They are a separate leaderboard worth no main-board
points, the templates are one-shot with no edits, and the main leaderboard is where the money is.
Worth running later as a focused push, once sign-ups are done.
