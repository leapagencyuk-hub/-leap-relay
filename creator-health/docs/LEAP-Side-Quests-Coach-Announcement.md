# Side Quests — coach announcement

For the coach channel. One post.

---

**SIDE QUESTS — STARTS SATURDAY**

Last week of every month, a handful of your creators get a personal target and seven nights to hit it. Hit it, they get their normal incentive **plus** bonus coins **plus** a Uni Wheel entry. Miss it, they lose nothing they had.

**Why we're doing it**

Every month a chunk of money sits one good week away from being real. Creators 40k short of holding a tier, or 50k short of ranking up, who drift over the line or don't depending on whether anyone said anything. Last month that was about £1,300 nobody chased.

This is us chasing it.

**How creators are picked**

The system does it, from the data, on the Saturday. It picks creators who are **close enough that seven nights can do it** and **not already smashing it** — if someone's going to clear the target anyway we're not paying coins for it. Anyone who hasn't been LIVE in three days is skipped too; a target they can't reach is just a card that lies to them.

Roughly 15-20 across the whole network. You'll get 1-4.

**What you have to do**

**1.** When the quest lands, **send your creator the message.** It's written for you, in the card, ready to copy. Don't rewrite it — the numbers in it are theirs and they're exact.

**2.** **Tell them to tell their room.** Creators who bring their community into it hit these far more often than the ones who go quiet about it.

**3.** **Check in at the halfway card and the last push.** You get three cards in here across the week, not seven. If a name on yours hasn't moved, that's the one worth a message.

That's it. You're not tracking anything — the system does the counting.

**What you'll get, and when**

| when | where | what |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturday** | your team room | your creators, their targets, the message to send |
| **Tuesday** | your team room | halfway — who's moved, who hasn't been touched |
| **Friday** | your team room | last night — who still needs you |
| **daily** | #sidequest-terminal | every quest across the network, one card |
| **daily** | #sidequest-updates | one card per creator, full detail |

The two Side Quest channels update every day if you want to watch it live. Your own room only pings you three times, so it stays worth reading.

**One thing worth knowing**

The target is **frozen the moment it's set**. It doesn't move if they have a good night or a bad one. So when you tell a creator the number, that number is good for the whole week — you'll never have to go back and change it on them.

Questions in here.

## NOTES

**The three cards are Saturday / Tuesday / Friday** on a quest set the 24th:
launch on the day, midpoint with four nights left, last push with one.
"Day 5" and "two days before the end" are the same evening on a seven-night
quest, so they are spread instead. Both days are set in
`config.sideQuests.teamCards` and can move without touching code.

**Channel names in the table are placeholders** — swap them for whatever the
two Side Quest channels are actually called.

**"About £1,300 nobody chased"** is September's missed value from the
simulation, rounded down. Real and defensible if a coach asks.

**The 15-20 figure** comes from September's 17 and October's current 17. If a
month comes out much larger, soften it rather than leave a number that stops
being true.
