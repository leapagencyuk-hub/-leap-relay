# Side Quests — launch announcement (creator-facing)

For #announcements in the creator server. One post.

---

⚡ **INTRODUCING: SIDE QUESTS**

Something new, starting this month.

**What they are**

In the last week of every month, a small number of creators get picked for a Side Quest — a personal target, sent straight to you, with 7 nights to hit it.

Hit it and you get:
✅ Your normal incentive, exactly as always
✅ **PLUS bonus coins on top**
✅ **PLUS 1 entry to the monthly Uni Wheel**

Nothing is taken away. This is extra.

**You can't apply for one**

You get picked. In the last week of the month we look at where everyone is and pick the creators sitting closest to something worth chasing — a rank up, holding your tier, a bonus you've already half earned.

If you get one, it's because we think you can do it.

**If you're already smashing your month, you probably won't get one.** You don't need it, and you're earning more than the quest is worth anyway.

**How many?**

Not many. Run against last month's numbers it would have gone to fewer than 20 creators across the whole network. It's meant to feel like being picked, because it is.

**If you get one**

You'll get a DM with your target, what it works out at per night, and exactly what you'll earn. Read it properly — the number is real and it's yours.

Then tell your room. Creators who bring their community along hit these far more often than the ones who go it alone. Your people want to be part of something.

Stuck, or not sure it's doable? Your coach is on it — open a ticket any time.

**First quests go out in the last week of October.** 🏆

Good luck.

---

## NOTES

**Why it says "closest to something worth chasing" and never "behind".** If
creators work out that quests go to people who are short of a target, some will
go quiet on purpose in the last fortnight to get one. The wording is true — a
quest only fires when somebody is within reach of a tier line — without
teaching anyone to sandbag.

**The line about already smashing it is doing real work.** The selection skips
creators already running well ahead of their own target, so your best people
genuinely won't get one. Better they read why here than wonder why they were
left out.

**"Fewer than 20" is from the September simulation** — 18 cards across 876
creators. If the October run comes out much bigger, soften it to "a small
number" rather than leave a figure that no longer holds.

**Still unpriced:** the Uni Wheel entry. It's in the announcement and in every
card, and nobody has told me what the prize costs. Worth knowing before 18
entries a month start going out.
