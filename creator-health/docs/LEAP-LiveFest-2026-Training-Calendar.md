## LEAP's position

Our creators are gaming creators. They will not engage with battle mechanics, so the Power
League arms race is not where we spend our coaching effort. We go hard on:

1. **Sign-up and Warm-Up** — free points, no battling, everyone can do it.
2. **Talent League (Gaming audition)** — the track built for what our creators already do.
3. **Fandom League** — fan-driven, not battle-driven.
4. **Daily Quest** (20 Nov – 21 Dec) — repeatable, no opponent needed.

Power League is covered so we can answer questions, not pushed.
