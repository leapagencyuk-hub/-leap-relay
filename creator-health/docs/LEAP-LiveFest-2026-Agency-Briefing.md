# TikTok LIVE FEST 2026 — first agency briefing

Source: the 41-minute agency briefing call on Google Meet, 23 October 2026, 16:00 UK
(125–131 agencies on the call). Presented by TikTok LIVE's EMEA team. Everything below is
read directly off the slides, with the spoken detail from the full transcript.

This was billed as "the very, very first briefing". A separate Talent League session runs
29 October, and the rest of the training calendar is in
`LEAP-LiveFest-2026-Training-Calendar.md`.

## 3. Warm-Up — 6 to 19 November

This is the phase that matters most to us. It is mission-based, no battling, and *"designed so
that pretty much anyone can complete it."*

**Warm-Up points carry over 1:1 into the Power League Round 1 leaderboard.** A creator who
finishes Warm-Up on 2,500 points starts Round 1 with 2,500 points banked before a single gift
is sent.

### The six missions

**4 single missions — complete once**

| Mission | Points | Cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign up for the campaign (tap the campaign page) | +100 | 0/1 |
| Invite other creators to sign up | +25 each | 0/10 |
| Learn how the FEST campaign works (browse the gameplay one-pager, 5 modules) | +20 each | 0/5 |
| Subscribe to campaign reminders (Campaign Center → Subscribe) | +50 | 0/1 |

Maximum from single missions: **500 points**.

**2 daily missions — repeat daily**

| Mission | Points | Cap | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Post a video | +100 | 0/2 daily | Hashtag **#LIVEFEST2026** AND the video uses the FEST effect |
| Go LIVE with the FEST effect | +50 | 0/1 daily | LIVE for more than 25 minutes AND wearing the FEST effect |

Maximum per day: **250 points**. Over 14 days of Warm-Up that is 3,500, plus 500 single = 4,000
available against a 2,500 target. **Every creator on our roster can hit the top tier.**

### Warm-Up rewards

| Condition | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Signed up | Early Points + FEST image stickers |
| 600 points | 1 Promo coupon — 50% off, up to $10 off |
| 1,500 points | **1 Double Blast** (saveable all the way to the Global Finale) |
| 2,500 points | Share of a **10,000,000 Diamond Pool**, split among all eligible creators at midnight on **19 November** |

The 10M diamond pool is the pitch for everyone who will never touch a leaderboard: *"it is a
direct reward that they can receive without having to climb a leaderboard or to beat anyone."*

The 1,500-point Double Blast was the one they told every agency to chase for every creator on
the roster, because it can be banked and spent later.

### Talent League audition — the gaming gate

To be eligible for the Talent League, a creator has to qualify during Warm-Up:

- **Gaming: at least 50% of their LIVE streams in the past 14 days must be gaming-related,
  measured at the time of selection.**
- Music: Music Lover or Music Talent status, earned by producing music LIVE streams.
- (Dancing also exists as a track: at least 3 dancing-related LIVE streams in the past 7 days.)

**This is the single most important line in the deck for LEAP.** The Talent League has a Gaming
track built for exactly what our creators already do, and the only gate is a 14-day gaming
streak ratio we can measure and fix now.

### Where creators actually find LIVE Fest

Inside LIVE: the banner in the LIVE room, the gift panel, the fan club panel, Top LIVE.
Outside LIVE: search "LIVE Fest" in the app, the Income+ tab, system notifications, the feed
card, and the My Page entry.

Their warning: *"don't assume that every creator will find it alone, especially those new ones.
Show them the entry route during your briefing."*

Battle-driven. Our creators will not play it the way TikTok intends, but we have to know it.

| Track | R1 → R2 | R2 → R3 | R3 → Global |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legend | 180 | 90 | 10 → Global Power Legend |
| Crown A | 180 | 90 | } |

| Crown F | 1500 | 120 | } 20 → Global Power Crown |
| Rising | 2000 | 100 | 10 → Global Power Rising |

Finish inside the quota and you advance; rank 181 in a 180 slot does not.

### Track division rule

- Tracks are set by **highest monthly revenue (diamonds) across a 6-month window**.
- **Cutoff: 17 November, 23:59:59 UTC+0.**
- UKI runs **8 tracks**. 27 regions participate worldwide, 10 of them in the EU.

Their instruction: agencies can and should predict which track each creator will land in and
start strategising now — but **do not tell the creator their track until it is published.**

### Basic points

| Source | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| **5 FEST gifts** — FEST Pop (1 coin), FEST Gear (99), FEST Cheers (299), FEST Party (2,999), a fifth not yet announced (will be the most expensive) | 1 coin = **2 points** |
| **3 Vault gifts** — 14,999 / 25,999 / 42,999 coins | 1 coin = **2 points** |
| Regular gifts | 1 coin = 1 point |
| Vote | 1 vote = 1 point |

The premium is the whole point: a FEST Party at 2,999 coins scores **5,998** points where a
regular gift at the same price scores 2,999. On one big gift that is a 3,000-point swing.

### Bonus points

**1. Double Blast — doubles points for 30 minutes.** Four ways to get one:

| How | Validity | Rounds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily unlock based on diamonds earned that day — R1&2: Legend 1,000 / Crown A–C 500 / Crown D–F 100 / Rising 50. R3: Legend 1,000 / Crown 500 / Rising 50 | **Expires at 24:00 the same day** | 1–3 |
| Rank in the Daily Top 20 Creator Leaderboard, Round 1 days 1–4 | Until the Global Finale ends | 1 |
| Advance Round 1 → Round 2 | Until the Global Finale ends | 1–2 |
| Advance Round 2 → Round 3 | Until the Global Finale ends | 2–3 |

Only one card can be active at a time. Activated at 23:50 it still runs 30 minutes past
midnight. Double points apply to gift points and votes.

Three of the four are saveable, so the real decision is *when* to burn them. Their note from
previous years: a lot of creators never unlock or never use their Double Blast at all. That is
free points being left on the table and it is an agency job to stop it.

**2. Bonus Hour — Round 1 only (20–24 Nov), 21:00–22:00 local time.** Every creator earns
double points in that window. Fixed start and end: go live at 21:50 and it still ends at 22:00.
**A Double Blast cannot stack on top of a Bonus Hour — they cancel, and you lose the card.**
So: do not schedule a Double Blast between 21:00 and 22:00 in Round 1, and do push FEST gifting
into that hour.

**3. Match bonus — Rounds 2 and 3 only.** The creator's daily highest Match score is added to
their leaderboard as a bonus, 1:1.

- Only a complete Match counts (5 minutes).
- At most 10,000,000 bonus points per day.
- Unlimited tries; only the single highest score counts.
- It is **not cumulative** — a later 20,000 match replaces a 10,000 match, it does not add to it
  (you gain the 10,000 difference).
- A cross-day match counts for the day it **ends**, not the day it started.
- Match bonus and Double Blast **do** take effect separately — that pair does stack. Gifts
  inside the match double; the match-bonus multiplier itself does not.

### Points

| Source | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 5 FEST gifts (same five as Power League) | 1 coin = 2 points |
| Regular gifts | 1 coin = 1 point |
| **Each unique gifter** | **100 points** |
| Vote | 1 vote = 1 point |

**Round 1 only has a daily gifting-amount cap of 300,000** on FEST and regular gifts. Round 2
has no equivalent cap.

The unique-gifter rule is the whole design: breadth of community beats depth of wallet. Twenty
people sending a 1-coin FEST Pop is 2,000 points from gifters alone before the gift value even
counts.

### Bonus points

- **Double Blast** — all rounds, 30 minutes. Daily unlock thresholds are by **unique gifters**,
  not diamonds: Legend 20, Gold 8, Silver 5, and so on down. Same Daily Top 20 rule, same
  advance-and-keep rule as Power League.
- **FEST Gift Match bonus** — Rounds 1 and 2. Unlimited tries of Specific-Gift Matches using
  the **1-coin FEST Pop only**. The daily highest match score is **multiplied by 5** and added
  to the leaderboard.
  - Only a complete Specific-Gift Match with the 1-coin FEST Gift counts.
  - A Vault gift sent during that match **does not count** toward it.
  - Example from the call: a match where 10,000 FEST Pops land scores 20,000 match points,
    ×5 = **100,000 bonus points**.
  - There is now a **batch gift function** so viewers can send in multiples instead of tapping
    10,000 times.

Their instruction: **pick one match per day**, brief the community on when to send, and treat
that match as the highest-leverage moment of the day.

## 8. TikTok's two Agency Top Tips

### 01 — BRIEF, DON'T JUST BROADCAST

*"Make sure creators actually understand LIVE FEST."* — **RUN IT LIVE**

- **Run a LIVE call** — host a short LIVE briefing or group call instead of just forwarding posters.
- **Explain the why** — what LIVE Fest is, why it matters, which opportunities actually apply to them.
- **Show the path** — show the campaign page, how to sign up, where to find missions.
- **Close the loop** — ask creators to repeat the gameplay back so you know they understood.

> ENSURE: every creator has been briefed and knows how to get started.

Said out loud: *"please don't simply forward all the campaign information and posters that we
throw at you… just because the creators have received a message, it doesn't necessarily mean
that they've read it, understood it, or know what they're supposed to do."*

Their challenge to every agency: **every creator gets a proper initial briefing before
6 November.**

### 02 — MAKE IT PERSONAL

*"One briefing does not fit every creator."* — **COACH BY TIER**

- **Segment first** — group creators by tier, strengths and likely league eligibility.
- **Top / pre-top** — align on rounds, planned LIVE sessions and the strategic tools that matter.
- **Mature / G4+** — focus on early momentum, community growth and consistency from day one.
- **Rising + Talent** — set simple mission goals and check Music / Gaming eligibility early.

> ENSURE: each creator understands their opportunity and has a realistic goal.

## 9. What this means for LEAP

We are a gaming agency. Our creators will not engage with Power League battle mechanics, and
pretending otherwise wastes the campaign. TikTok's own slide says not to push everyone into
Power by default, so we are not fighting the grain — we are following it.

### Where we actually play

1. **Warm-Up, for every single creator, no exceptions.** 2,500 points is reachable on missions
   alone, it needs no opponent, it pays a share of 10M diamonds, it banks a Double Blast, and
   every point carries 1:1 into Power League Round 1. This is the highest return-per-effort
   thing in the whole campaign and it is pure compliance — sign up, post two videos a day with
   #LIVEFEST2026 and the FEST effect, go live 25+ minutes with the effect.
2. **Talent League Gaming track.** The gate is 50% gaming LIVE streams in the past 14 days. Our
   creators are already gaming creators; we just need to make sure the ratio holds at selection
   and that they actually complete the track selection during Warm-Up.
3. **Fandom League.** Scored on unique gifters, not whale depth, and tracked by active fan club
   size. We hold fan club numbers in the daily export, so we can band every creator now and
   work the bands until the 6 December lock.
4. **Daily Quest.** Repeatable, solo, 3 missions a day, and 6 missions banks another Double
   Blast. No opponent required.

Power League gets covered so coaches can answer questions and so our bigger creators who *do*
want to rank have the mechanics right. It does not get pushed.

### What we have to build

- **The tracker.** TikTok said it twice, and it is the thing we already have most of. Per
  creator: priority league, Warm-Up points, missions done, Double Blasts held and when to spend
  them, Fandom band, Talent eligibility, current rank and the gap to the cut.
- **Fandom band prediction** off active fan club members, against the UKI+ table above.
- **Talent Gaming eligibility**: percentage of gaming LIVE streams over the last 14 days.
- **Warm-Up compliance**: has the creator signed up, subscribed to reminders, invited creators,
  posted today, gone live 25+ minutes with the effect.
