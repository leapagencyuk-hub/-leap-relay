**LEAP Initiative Requirements**

Joining Leap is simple.

There are no entry requirements to join Leap other than:

- You create gaming content
- You are 18 years or older

**Your 90-Day Journey**

Once you join, your 90-day onboarding period begins.

During this time, your goal is to learn the platform, make use of Leap's
resources, and build your community while working towards our minimum
activity requirements.

**What you need to hit**

Three things, spread across your first 90 days:

**1.** Go LIVE within your first **7 days**
**2.** Go LIVE on **10 days** in your first 30
**3.** Earn **10,000 Diamonds** in your first 90

**Finish early**

Pass 10,000 Diamonds at any point and your 90 days end there and then. You
move from New Creator to Creator, and the 10-day requirement stops applying.
Go hard in week one and you'll never think about the clock again.

**Why we set these**

They're roughly what TikTok itself expects from anyone trying to build a career
on LIVE. They are the minimum, not the target.

They also tell us something. Creators who go LIVE on 10 days in their first
month are around four times more likely to still be growing two months later
than the ones who don't. The bar is where it is because that's where the
difference shows up.

**You'll always know where you stand**

You'll hear from us on day one, and again any time you're behind on something,
with exactly what's left and how long you've got. Nobody is removed without
knowing it was coming.

**If you don't meet them**

You'll come off the creator network. It isn't permanent and it isn't personal —
if you want another go, just ask, and you'll start again from day one with the
same support as everyone else.

**Struggling with any of it?**

Open a ticket in #support. Setup trouble, no idea where to start, nerves, or
something going on in life that's getting in the way — tell us **before** your
deadline and we'll work with you. After it, our hands are tied.

This gives you three full months to understand TikTok LIVE, improve your
content, and let Leap help you grow.

We're here to support you every step of the way.

## NOTES

**The 30 hours is gone.** The current post asks for 30 hours of LIVE streaming
in 90 days. The new rule 2 asks for 10 LIVE days in the first 30 instead, which
is a different shape of commitment — it's about turning up regularly and early
rather than clocking hours by the deadline. If you want the 30 hours kept as a
fourth bar, say so, but be aware it is a separate thing to police and it is not
one the data was tested against.

**"Four times more likely" is real.** Creators who cleared 10 LIVE days in
their first 30 were 59-71% still streaming two months on, against 15-19% for
those who didn't. Cut that paragraph if you'd rather not publish retention
numbers.

works, but if the artwork lists the old 30 hours / 10,000 diamonds anywhere it
will contradict the text underneath it.
