**NEW CREATOR SETUP — live 1 Nov**

**THE REQUIREMENTS**
Every creator who joins gets 90 days to clear three bars:
**1.** Go LIVE within 7 days of joining
**2.** Go LIVE on 10 days in their first 30
**3.** Earn 10,000 diamonds in their first 90

Miss any one of them and they're removed.

**THE ROLES**
`NEW CREATOR` — given the day they join, carries the 90-day clock
`CREATOR` — full roster member, no clock

**THE ROLE SWITCH**
The bot swaps `NEW CREATOR` → `CREATOR` on whichever comes first:
• They pass **10,000 diamonds** at any point. Clock stops there and then, and rule 2 stops applying to them — someone who goes hard in week one never thinks about the day count again.
• They reach **day 90** having cleared all three.

The bot DMs these automatically. You see each one fire in your channel, so you always know what your creators have been told.
• **Day 1** — welcome, your clock starts today, here's your coach

• **Day 25** — only if under 10 days: "you're on N of 10, 5 days left"
• **On hitting 10,000** — "you're done, you're a CREATOR now"
• **Day 80** — only if under 10,000: "you're on N, that's X a night"
• **Removal** — below

**1.** Bot DMs them the removal message — which rule, that it isn't personal, and that they can come back
**2.** `NEW CREATOR` role removed, server access dropped
**3.** Removed from the agency in Backstage — <WHO DOES THIS>
