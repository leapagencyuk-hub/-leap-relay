# LEAP x TikTok LIVE FEST 26 — brand kit for the LIVE Fest tracker

Where it lives: `creator-health/public/brand/livefest-tokens.css` is the file the app
imports. `creator-health/public/brand/index.html` is the spec sheet and the living demo,
served at `/brand/`.

## The one idea

**LEAP owns the surface. LIVE FEST owns the signal.**

Everything structural is navy and LEAP blue: the page, the cards, the chrome, the
buttons that are not about the campaign. Cyan and pink mean a LIVE Fest number and
nothing else. The point is that a coach skimming a page can tell a Fest figure from an
ordinary diamond total before reading the label, and that a screenshot still reads as
LEAP rather than as TikTok.

## Where the colours came from

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **#25F4EE** Fest Cyan | TikTok's published brand cyan |
| **#FE2C55** Fest Red | TikTok's published brand red |
| **#EF62AC** Fest Pink | Sampled from the campaign key art in the 23 and 26 October recordings |
| **#C33264** Fest Magenta | Sampled from the same key art |
| **#004AAD** LEAP Blue | The logo blue, from @leapagencyuk |

The sampled values came back at #37FAF5 and #FE296A, within a shade of TikTok's
published pair, so the published values win. Their own assets will use those, and ours
should match when the two sit side by side.

TikTok's own typeface, **TikTok Sans**, is open licensed, so it is legitimate to load on
campaign surfaces. Everything falls back to the system stack, which resolves to SF on a
Mac or an iPhone. That fallback is most of where the Apple feel comes from.

## The rules that matter

**The arc appears twice at most.** The campaign band at the top of a page, and the one
primary Fest action on it. Progress bars and figures use flat Fest Cyan. Spread the
gradient across every bar and it stops being a signal and becomes wallpaper.

**Cyan means earned.** If a number did not come from LIVE Fest, it is not cyan.

**Fest Red is a deadline, not an error.** Behind uses #FF5F6D, the portal's existing
risk colour. Fest Red is reserved for a window closing.

**No money on a coach surface.** Carried straight over from the portal. Coins yes,
pounds no.

**Numbers are tabular, always.** A tracker that updates every morning cannot have a
column shifting by a pixel.

**Depth is a hairline border and one step on the surface ramp.** Not a drop shadow.
That is the whole trick behind the iOS look, and it is why the shadow tokens are almost
empty.

## What is in the token file

Surface ramp, LEAP blues, the four Fest colours and the arc, the four state colours
shared with the portal, a six-step type scale with its line heights and tracking, a 4pt
space scale, four radii, two elevations, and motion with a reduced-motion override.
