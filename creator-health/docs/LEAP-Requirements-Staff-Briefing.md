# Staff briefing — new creator requirements

Post to the coach channel. Discord caps a message at 2,000 characters, so this
is written as five posts. Keep them in order and pin the first.

---

## POST 1 — what is changing

**NEW CREATOR REQUIREMENTS — read this properly, it changes your roster**

From **<DATE>**, every creator who joins LEAP has 90 days to clear three bars:

**1.** Go LIVE within 7 days of joining
**2.** Go LIVE on 10 days in their first 30
**3.** Earn 10,000 diamonds in their first 90

Miss one and they come off the roster.

**The shortcut:** pass 10,000 diamonds at any point and they are done early. The
clock stops, NEW CREATOR comes off, CREATOR goes on, and rule 2 stops applying
to them. A creator who goes hard never has to think about the day count.

Removal is not a ban. Anyone removed can rejoin and restart at day one. Say
that to them when the conversation happens, because it is true and it matters.

---

## POST 2 — why we are doing this

We tested these rules against our own data before setting them, not after.

**They catch the right people.** 58 creators would have been removed in
September. Between them they earned **13,587 diamonds all month** — 0.05% of the
network. In the first week of October those 58 produced 874 diamonds between
them, and nine had already quit without being asked.

**The bar predicts who makes it.** Creators who clear 10 LIVE days in their
first 30 are **four times more likely** to still be streaming two months later,
and more than **ten times** as likely to be earning properly. The ones who do
not, mostly stop altogether.

**This is not about saving money.** Removing all 58 would have cost us a twentieth
of one percent of the network. It is about where your attention goes. Sur3shot is
carrying 230 creators. Nobody can coach 230 people, and a large part of that list
has never gone LIVE once.

## POST 4 — how it rolls out

**Week 1.** Requirements posted and pinned in the creator server. Membership
screening turned on, so every new creator ticks them before they can post.
Everyone joining from <DATE> gets NEW CREATOR and starts their clock.

**Week 1 to 4.** Existing roster gets a grace window. Nobody who joined before
<DATE> is removed in the first month. They get the same countdown messages so
they know where they stand, and the rules bite on them from <DATE + 30>.

**From week 5.** Rules apply to everybody. Expect roughly **70 to 80 removals a
month** in steady state once all three deadlines are running, against about 105
signings.

That last number is the one to sit with. We are not going to be a smaller
agency — we are going to be the same size with a roster that actually streams.
But it means recruitment stops being measured on signings and starts being
measured on **signings that clear day 30**. Right now that is 31%.
