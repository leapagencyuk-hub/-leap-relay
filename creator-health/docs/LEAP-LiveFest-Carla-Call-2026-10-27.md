## 1. The thing that changes the deck

Perry put the problem to Carla directly:

> As a gamer who's been on the app for two months, or two years, I look at that and I
> think there's no point in me getting involved until December. In hindsight there's
> actually no point in a gaming creator signing up or doing the Warm Up, because none of
> that goes to the Talent League.

The answer they landed on, and this is the thesis the creator deck should be built
around:

> Yes there's a Power League and yes there's loads of battles in there, but they've even
> made side leagues for gamers for you to get involved with. We can use this whole month
> to revenue boost, to build traffic, to build the LIVE Fest event, so when December
> comes and the Talent League comes, our communities already know what it's about,
> because we just spent the whole month grinding LIVE Fest ready for the big Talent
> League.
>
> **Treat November as an entire warm up slash revenue boosting and traffic opportunity,
> to get your community rallied up and part of LIVE Fest. And if you don't get in the top
> 100 in the Power League, it's not the end of the world, because the Talent League is in
> December. That is when we go full blown, big push, balloons and all that.**

That reframes the whole campaign for a gaming roster. November is not the event. November
is the run-up, and it pays in revenue and traffic while it builds the audience that wins
in December.

## 2. LEAP's agency track

LEAP has been placed in a soft league. Studio 42 and Impact went into the big one.

> We're in the easy league. We've lucked out. We could actually win our league and go to
> Tokyo. They did it on our three quiet months we just had over the summer.

That confirms the rule from the tournament deck: agency tracks are set on **July to
September average revenue**. LEAP's quiet summer put us in a bracket we can realistically
win.

## 3. Is LIVE Fest data exportable?

Asked directly. The answer appears to be **no**.

> That would change the whole game... Yeah, we'll pay for it... They'll come around
> eventually, I reckon. But people will abuse it, wouldn't they?

So the tracker cannot pull LIVE Fest points. Anything we track has to be computed from
the daily creator export or logged by a coach by hand.

## 7. What this changes for us

**The deck's thesis is now settled.** November is the warm-up, the revenue boost and the
traffic build. December is the event. Every slide should ladder to that rather than
treating the Power League as the main act.

**The Warm Up argument needs restating.** Warm Up points do not carry into the Talent
League, only into Power League Round 1. For a gaming creator the reason to do the Warm
Up is the 10 million diamond pool, the banked Double Blast, and the habit of streaming
with the effect on while the community learns what LIVE Fest is. Not the leaderboard.

**There are five TikTok gaming campaigns in November that have nothing to do with the
Power League.** House Cup, Loot Drop, 24 Hours of Miami, GGs Only and Gaming Arena.
That is the evidence for "they've made side leagues for gamers", and it is the strongest
answer to a creator who thinks November is dead for them.

**No data export.** The tracker computes everything from the daily export or from what a
coach logs. Worth confirming again at Daily Office Hours from 16 November, but plan as
though the answer is no.
